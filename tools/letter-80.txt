Dear Nicola,

I just wanted to write down a few thoughts on your 50th Birthday….

While we only met just last year, it feels like I have known you for so much longer.

It is so wonderful that a beautiful family from Dusseldorf Germany has ended up 16,000kms away from home on the other side of the world on the sunny Gold Coast!

Your two beautiful sons have blessed your life and I am so glad our boys became friends in 2025. I remember first meeting you at a bbq at Lachie's house when our boys were in grade 7. I was kind of in awe of you and the way you had just moved countries with two kids in tow, your husband still abroad, big beaming smile, seemingly managing to hold it all together, all for your family. My first thought of you was – Wow, what an amazingly brave woman you are!

Getting to know you better from then I also see what a kind soul you are – always warm, kind and generous. Always volunteering and being a wonderful mum to your boys. They should feel super proud and lucky to have you as their mum!

Your smile is so huge and you have the most beautiful sparkly unique eyes I have ever seen, they are so cool! I wish you loved them more than you do!

Not only are you beautiful on the outside you just seem to always radiate happiness and positivity also which I love. You are so funny, full of warmth and also have a really great sense of humor, which I find so reassuring that although people grow up on different sides of the world, they can have so many similarities.

I think you are probably the sort of person that doesn't even realise what a very special lady, mum, wife and friend you really are. So today I hope you take note!

Here is my little wish list for you on your 50th Birthday:
• Many more fabulous years of health and vitality for you and your beautiful family
• To be able to stay in Australia and have a long and happy life drinking champagne and running along the GC beaches for years to come.
• Hiking mountains (in appropriate footwear), challenging yourself to remain healthy fit and strong for a long happy life.

I feel so very fortunate to have met you Nicola, and am honoured to celebrate your 50 wonderful years on this earth with you today.

With much love, your friend, Cheree

PS: I have also just realized you are now old enough to compete in the Aussie Masters games and become our National pickleball champion!
