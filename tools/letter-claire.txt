Nicola – Origin and Meaning
A feminine form of Nicholas, from the ancient Greek Nikólaos. Nikē means victory; laós means people: "victory of the people". The name shares the tradition of Saint Nicholas, remembered for generosity. In Germany, his kindness is celebrated on Nikolaustag, 6 December.
